The PicoScope glossary

L

  • Lozenge: Refers to the rounded-corner rectangles on the PicoScope 7 screen. These can be clicked to open a configuration window.
    e.g. the Trigger lozenge can be clicked to open the Trigger configration panel

T

  • Timebase: The timebase is the time measurement for each X-axis division on the PicoScope graph. As the graph is split into 10 divisions, the timebase can be increased or decreased to show more or less time of the trace on the screen.
    e.g. A timebase of 1 ms/div shows more of the signal than 500 μs/div.
