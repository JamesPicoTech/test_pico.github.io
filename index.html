Welcome to the PicoScope Documentation

This user's guide is designed to help you use the PicoScope 7. With a scope device from Pico Technology, PicoScope turns your PC into a powerful PC oscilloscope and spectrum analyzer, with all the features and performance of a benchtop oscilloscope at a fraction of the cost.

For PicoScope products and tools, go to https://www.picotech.co.uk
